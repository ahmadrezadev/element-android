Mana Classic is the previous-generation of Mana mobile app. Friends, family and communities should use the free and open source Mana X app which is faster, easier to use, and more powerful. New users of public sector organisations, enterprises and professional teams users should use the Mana Pro app that is built for work and organisations. Mana Classic is available at least until the end of 2025 and will receive critical security updates but no further enhancements or new features.
